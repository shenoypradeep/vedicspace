Akka guides ayogya

Long long ago.... not so long ago, really - there lived an "ayogya" (useless, good-for-nothing) who grew up in Hyderabad and smashed the CET (engineering entrance) so hard that they ran out of paper to print his rank 😜.

After multiple knocks, at "reputed" colleges for management quota seats — which cost more than a multiple organs combined — and by 'ajji-punya' (grace of ancestors), particularly Ammembal Subbarao Pai 😜😜, Ayogya landed at the pious Canara Engineering College. The college, run by Konkani community, was offering a few management seats under its sacred "Konkani quota" reserved for students of Konkani community. Despite his stellar CET performance, he had his heart set on Electronics engineering, but (now) gladly, only a few Computer Science seats remained.

Ayogya’s name was being called out to come to the dias and accept the seat — the last candidate! — and now a big "dvandva" dawned upon him —

Ayogya, was still undecided (as if he had options!!) He had 2 choices: 1) Walk up to the dais, accept the remaining Computer Science seat, or 2) decline and walk away. No time to think.

In that defining moment, he intuitively reached into his dad’s bag, pulled out Shruti akka’s bio-data to check her engineering branch — and turned out it was Computer Science!

That was the sign, which made him walk to the dais, claim the seat, and by his third year, land a job with a global IT services giant, which continues to sustainan him to this day!! Just some of the life-changing things that akkas do . . .
