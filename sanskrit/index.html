A Practical 30-Day Start

The app is most useful when learners follow a rhythm. This plan gives a simple first month for self-study or guided Vedic Space circles.

Week 1

Train the ear

Work on vowels, aspiration, dental and retroflex contrasts, and steady recitation pace.

  • Use the beginner console daily.
  • Read 5 to 10 syllables aloud every day.
  • Shadow one spoken Sanskrit clip or class.
Week 2

Own the script

Move from recognition to reading. Pair Devanagari with transliteration until confidence builds.

  • Practice script conversion with Aksharamukha.
  • Read one short verse line aloud daily.
  • Memorize 5 useful spoken phrases.
Week 3

Build grammar

Introduce noun endings, pronouns, and present tense verbs through real sentence patterns.

  • Study one declension family and one verb pattern.
  • Write 5 tiny Sanskrit sentences.
  • Parse one short passage with help from tools.
Week 4

Enter real texts

Use reading platforms, dictionaries, and spoken repetition to make the language alive and connected.

  • Read a short stotra or subhashita with support.
  • Look up unfamiliar forms in Cologne Lexicon.
  • Choose your next level in the console.
